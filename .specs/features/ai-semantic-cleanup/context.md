# Locked decisions and assumptions

- "Off-topic" means speech unrelated to Salesforce, artificial intelligence,
  or the user's work; it is muted, not removed.
- Silence gaps longer than 2 seconds are compressed to 0.5 seconds.
- Timeline audio is rendered and transcribed locally with Whisper.
- Transcript segments, not audio/video files, are sent to the provider.
- The active timeline is the source of truth, so prior cuts and retimes are
  respected.
- The user reviews a plan before Apply; generation never mutates the project.
- Existing exact-duration retime behavior remains supported.
